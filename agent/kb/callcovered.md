# CallCovered knowledge base

Short, voice-ready reference articles for Jarvis. Each `##` section is one
searchable article. Keep answers short and spoken when quoting these.

## What CallCovered is

CallCovered is Alexandria's branded software product. Under the hood it is
GoHighLevel resold in SaaS Mode, white-labeled so customers never see the
HighLevel name. It is sold to owner-operated roofing contractors in South
Florida: Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. Typical buyer: 1 to 10
staff, the owner works on the roof or runs crews, no front-desk staff, losing
after-hours and weekend calls. Never mention GoHighLevel or HighLevel to a
customer; the product is CallCovered, full stop.

## The three tiers

Essential, $97 per month: branded software only. CRM, pipelines, calendar
booking, missed-call text-back, review requests. Do-it-yourself.

Growth, $297 per month: everything in Essential, plus Conversation AI for
SMS and chat follow-up, automated workflows, reputation management, and
storm-lead workflows.

Receptionist, $497 per month: everything in Growth, plus a Voice AI agent
that answers calls and books jobs 24/7. One-time done-for-you setup fee of
$997. This is the flagship tier and the best margin.

## The ROI sentence that closes

One saved roofing job pays for the top tier many times over. A full roof
replacement in South Florida runs $12,000 to $45,000 or more: shingle $12K
to $22K, tile $18K to $35K, metal $25K to $50K plus, with a 15 to 25 percent
premium from Miami-Dade and Broward wind codes. One saved $12,000 job covers
the $497 tier for two full years. Say it plainly: every missed call is a
five-figure job going to a competitor.

## The wedge: missed-call text-back

The foot in the door is the 90-second demo, not a feature tour. When the
roofer misses a call, CallCovered texts the caller back instantly: "Sorry we
missed you, how can we help?" That single automation rescues jobs that used
to die on voicemail. Lead every conversation with this, then the AI
receptionist, then the full stack. Sell "never miss another $20,000 job",
never sell software.

## Why roofing, why South Florida

Regulation creates demand no other niche has. Florida SB 2-D: insurers cannot
non-renew a policy solely because of roof age under 15 years; at 15-plus the
owner gets an inspection right, and five-plus years of remaining roof life
blocks non-renewal. That is a rolling, law-created inspection and replacement
pipeline. Add hurricane season storm-damage surges that overwhelm small shops'
phones, and Google LSA charging for unanswered business-hours calls as leads.
Every missed call now costs twice.

## Client onboarding stages

Every CallCovered client moves through these stages in order. Track them with
the client tools; never skip A2P.

1. intake: collect business name, owner name, phone, email, tier, services,
   hours, service area, current phone setup, website URL.
2. a2p: A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration in the Trust Center. HARD
   GATE: no texts go out until this is approved, or carriers filter them.
3. subaccount: the client's CallCovered sub-account is provisioned. In SaaS
   Mode this happens automatically on Stripe checkout; manual otherwise.
4. snapshot: deploy the roofing snapshot: pipelines, missed-call text-back,
   quote follow-ups, storm broadcasts, bilingual AI voice receptionist.
5. forwarding: call forwarding or the new tracking number is live and tested.
6. voice_config: AI voice agent configured: greeting script, business hours,
   after-hours behavior, Spanish-language handling.
7. test_call: a live test call is placed and the booking flow verified.
8. live: go-live. Client is active; move to check-in follow-ups.

Set reminders for check-ins: day 1 after go-live, day 3, day 7.

## A2P registration gate

Each client's roofing business must register its own brand and campaign use
cases in the GHL Trust Center before any texting. The carrier requires the
client's website to carry a privacy policy page and a terms page that mention
SMS messaging. No registration means texts get filtered or blocked. This is
the most common onboarding stall: start it on day one, chase it daily, and
never promise a go-live date until it clears.

## Compliance scripts

Cold B2B email is legal under CAN-SPAM: honest headers, opt-out link,
physical address. Cold marketing SMS needs prior express written consent
under the TCPA: $500 to $1,500 per text, no B2B exemption, so email-first
always; SMS only after the prospect opts in. AI voice counts as an
"artificial voice" under the TCPA. Florida is an all-party consent state, so
the AI greeting must disclose it is an AI assistant and that the call is
being recorded. Before any client scales outreach: LLC, E&O insurance, and an
attorney-reviewed contract with a limitation-of-liability clause.

## Handling common objections

"I already have a CRM": CallCovered replaces the CRM and the missed calls
with one system; ask how many calls went to voicemail last week.

"Too expensive": return to the ROI sentence. One saved job covers two years
of the top tier. Ask what one average job is worth to them.

"Does the AI sound robotic?": the voice is conversational and bilingual; the
90-second live demo answers this better than any claim. Offer the demo line.

"What if the AI messes up a call?": every call is recorded and transcribed;
the owner reviews them, and handoff rules send tricky calls to a human. The
AI only books straightforward jobs.

"I don't have time to set this up": that is exactly what the $997
done-for-you setup buys. We configure everything; they take a test call.

## Spanish-language note

South Florida is heavily Spanish-speaking, including many roofing customers
and crew leads. The AI receptionist handles Spanish callers. Mention this in
every demo; it is a differentiator Roofful does not lead with.

## Onboarding a new team member

New agents, human or AI, learn in this order: the product (tiers article),
the buyer (South Florida roofer article), the wedge demo script, the
onboarding stages, A2P and compliance, then shadowing. Give them the
missed-call text-back demo to perform themselves before they ever talk to a
prospect. Escalation rule: pricing exceptions, compliance questions, and
anything involving contracts go to Alexandria. Everything else, the agent
handles and logs.

## SaaS Mode mechanics

Customers check out through Stripe; SaaS Mode provisions their sub-account
automatically. Alexandria's cost base is the $497 per month Agency Pro plan
plus usage for calls, SMS, and AI. Gross margins run roughly 78 to 90
percent on the $297 tier and 60 to 80 percent on the $497 tier. Never sell a
tier where one heavy-usage client erases the margin. Breakeven on the
platform base is about three clients.
